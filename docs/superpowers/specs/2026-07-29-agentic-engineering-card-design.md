# Agentic engineering card

## Purpose

Present the f3dasm-agentic benchmarks as a serious research-infrastructure project: agents face unresolved engineering questions, expensive physical oracles, finite budgets, evidence gates, and a critic that can reject a claim.

## Narrative

The page opens with the distinction between a score and a discovery. Its concrete example is the rocking-mast metamaterial benchmark. The 5.9× Bessa numerical leader is shown as progress, not as the benchmark answer, because it does not provide the required new mechanism.
